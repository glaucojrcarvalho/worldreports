candidate `country.yaml` byte-for-byte, to exercise registry, routes,
rendering, full site, catalogue, source isolation and browser tests.

remains unchanged and unregistered.

English and Portuguese, 12 source-provenance mappings and visual
contact sheets, then builds the entire atlas and runs all Python and
Node regressions. The workflow is read-only and preserves artifacts.
